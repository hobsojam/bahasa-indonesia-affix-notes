# Appendix A: Affix Index

A single alphabetical reference table for every affix in scope for this
book. Pulled from `pembilas/data/affixes.json` (the prefixes/suffixes/
circumfixes) plus the infix set, which pembilas doesn't currently model.
"Status" tracks whether the linked chapter is drafted yet — this table
will be kept in sync as chapters land.

| Affix | Type | Function | Chapter | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *-an* | suffix | Result noun, instrument noun, or collective | 9 | not drafted |
| *ber-* | prefix | Intransitive verb; to have or use something | 6 | not drafted |
| *ber-...-an* | circumfix | Reciprocal action or plural action | 6 | not drafted |
| *di-* | prefix | Passive verb (patient focus) | 5 | drafted |
| *-el-, -em-, -er-* (infixes) | infix | Small closed, mostly fossilized set (e.g. *gelembung*, *gemuruh*, *telunjuk*) | 11 | not drafted |
| *-i* (bare) | suffix | Locative or repeated action on object, without *me-* | 7 | not drafted |
| *-kan* (bare) | suffix | Causative or benefactive, without *me-* | 7 | not drafted |
| *ke-* | prefix | Ordinal numbers; some nominals | 10 | not drafted |
| *ke-...-an* | circumfix | Abstract noun or state noun | 9 | not drafted |
| *-lah* | suffix | Softens imperatives; adds emphasis | 10 | not drafted |
| *me-* | prefix | Active verb (actor focus) | 3 | drafted |
| *me-...-i* | circumfix | Directional or repeated active verb | 4 | not drafted |
| *me-...-kan* | circumfix | Causative active verb | 4 | not drafted |
| *-nya* | suffix | Definiteness marker or nominaliser | 10 | not drafted |
| *pe-* | prefix | Agent or instrument noun | 8 | not drafted |
| *pe-...-an* | circumfix | Abstract noun or place noun | 9 | not drafted |
| *per-...-an* | circumfix | Abstract or collective noun (lexically distinct from *pe-...-an*, not an allomorph of it) | 9 | not drafted |
| *se-* | prefix | One; same; as...as | 10 | not drafted |
| *ter-* | prefix | Accidental or stative passive (also a separate superlative use on adjective roots — see Ch. 5) | 5 | drafted |

Not yet in this table: the particles *-kah*, *-pun*, *-tah* mentioned in
the Chapter 10 outline entry — add them here once that chapter is
drafted and their exact function/notes are settled.
